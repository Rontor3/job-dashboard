\textbf{Optimization of Passive Equity Investments}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Developed an asset selection and allocation model for ETF investments.
    \item Used Historical Price and PE/PB ratio data and ran Monte Carlo Simulations to determine the parameters.
    \item Backtested the strategy on the last 5 years and got 4\% excess XIRR over the actual portfolio.
    \item Deployed the model to production after successful UAT; the strategy continues to outperform the actual portfolio.
\end{itemize}
